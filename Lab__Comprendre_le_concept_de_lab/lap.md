# What Is a Lab?

A lab is a practical learning activity that allows learners to apply their theoretical knowledge through exercises, experiments, or guided activities.

## Objective

A lab helps learners develop practical skills, solve problems, and better understand the concepts they study.

## Procedure

Generally, the instructor explains the objectives and instructions. Then, learners complete the assigned activities, test their results, correct their mistakes, and draw conclusions.

## Role in Practical Learning

A lab connects theory with practice. It helps learners strengthen their knowledge, develop independence, and gain practical experience.
