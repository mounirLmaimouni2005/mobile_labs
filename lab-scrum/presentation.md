# 2. Definition of Scrum

**Scrum is a lightweight framework that helps teams organize their work and develop a product step by step.**


---
<!-- I -->

# 3. Problem Statement and Solution

## The Problem
When teams work on a project, they can have different problems:

- Tasks are not well organized.
- Project needs can change.
- Communication can be difficult.
- The team may not know its progress.
---
## The Solution with Scrum

Scrum helps teams by:

- Organizing tasks.
- Dividing work into short Sprints.
- Communicating regularly.
- Checking results.
- Making changes when needed.

---

# 4. Fundamentals of Scrum

**1. Transparency**

Everyone can see and understand the work.

**2. Inspection**

The team checks the work and progress.

**3. Adaptation**

The team makes changes when needed.


---

# 5. Components of Scrum

## A. Accountabilities

**1. Product Owner**

**2. Scrum Master**

**3. Developers**

---
## B. Scrum Events

Scrum has five events:

**1. Sprint:** A short period of work, one month or less.

**2. Sprint Planning:** The team plans the work for the Sprint.

**3. Daily Scrum:** A 15-minute meeting for Developers to check progress and plan their work.

**4. Sprint Review:** The team checks the product with stakeholders.

**5. Sprint Retrospective:** The team discusses how to improve its work.

---
## C. Scrum Artifacts

Scrum has three main artifacts:

**1. Product Backlog**

A list of all the work needed for the product.

**2. Sprint Backlog**

The work selected for the current Sprint, with a plan to complete it.

**3. Increment**

A usable part of the product that meets the Definition of Done.

rement → **Definition of Done**

---

# 6. How Scrum Works and Conclusion

## How Scrum Works

Scrum works in short periods called Sprints.

1. The Product Owner manages the Product Backlog.
2. During Sprint Planning, the team plans the Sprint.
3. Developers work on the selected tasks.
4. Developers check their progress every day.
5. At the end, the team reviews the result.
6. The team discusses how to improve.
7. A new Sprint starts.

---
## Conclusion

Scrum helps teams organize their work, communicate, and develop products step by step.

It also helps teams learn from their experience and improve their work.
